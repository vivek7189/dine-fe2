DineOpen vs Petpooja: Which POS is better for restaurants?

DineOpen and Petpooja both offer restaurant POS software in India. Both handle GST billing, KOT, menus and inventory. DineOpen starts at ₹899/month with a 7-day free trial and no card. Petpooja lists its Base plan at ₹12,000 + taxes and syncs Swiggy and Zomato orders directly, which DineOpen does not.

What is the main difference between DineOpen and Petpooja?

Price and aggregator sync. DineOpen is a low monthly price with the captain app, kitchen display and QR ordering included. Petpooja lists a ₹12,000 + taxes Base plan, and its Operations Manager Growth plan (₹20,000 + taxes, includes Base) adds captain app, KDS and QR ordering.

Petpooja is a large, established company with a wide partner network, and it pulls Swiggy and Zomato orders straight into the POS. DineOpen does not do that today. If most of your orders come from aggregators, that matters.

Is DineOpen better than Petpooja for small restaurants?

For many single-outlet restaurants and cafés, yes on price. DineOpen is ₹899/month and you can try it free for 7 days without a card. It runs on phones, tablets, Windows PCs and in a browser.

If you need automatic Swiggy/Zomato order sync or in-person setup, Petpooja may suit you better. Ask Petpooja for a written quote, including the billing period, before you compare.
