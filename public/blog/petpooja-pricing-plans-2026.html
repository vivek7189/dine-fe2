2. Is Petpooja Priced Per Year or Per Month?

The official page doesn't say, and it doesn't say whether the price is per outlet. We have not seen Petpooja confirm either point publicly, so we don't guess here. Ask before you sign.

Questions to ask Petpooja before you buy

  • Is the price per year, and is it per outlet?
  • What is the renewal price in year two?
  • Which hardware (printer, tablet, cash drawer) do I need to buy separately?
  • Is there a monthly payment option?
