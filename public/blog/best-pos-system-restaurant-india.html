2. What to Look For in a Restaurant POS in India

  • GST billing: correct CGST/SGST on every bill, inclusive or exclusive pricing per item, GST reports.
  • KOT and kitchen flow: KOTs printed or shown on a kitchen display, split by station.
  • Captain/waiter app: waiters take orders at the table on their phone.
  • QR ordering: guests order from the table; orders land on the POS.
  • Inventory: stock reduces with recipes; low-stock alerts.
  • Aggregators: do you need Swiggy/Zomato orders synced automatically, or is manual entry fine?
  • Pricing clarity: is the price public, monthly or yearly, per outlet, and what costs extra?
  • Devices: does it run on the Android tablet, phone or PC you already have?
